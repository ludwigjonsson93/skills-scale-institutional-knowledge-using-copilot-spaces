# OctoAcme Project Management Docs: Process Summary and Links

## Process summary

OctoAcme runs projects through a consistent lifecycle that begins with **initiation** and ends with learning and continuous improvement. Initiation validates the business need, objective, measurable success metrics, stakeholders, high-level timeline, risks, and resource needs before work moves into planning. The Project Manager coordinates delivery and the Product Manager prioritizes outcomes and backlog value, while developers, QA, and stakeholders contribute expertise and decisions.

During **planning**, the team holds a kickoff, creates a prioritized backlog with acceptance criteria, estimates effort, identifies dependencies and risks, and agrees on a Definition of Done and release milestones. Execution is iterative: work moves through a visible project board, daily standups and weekly delivery syncs surface progress and blockers, pull requests receive review, and automated and manual quality checks validate readiness. Metrics, dashboards, risk registers, and stakeholder updates provide transparency and drive escalation when needed.

Before **release**, the team confirms that acceptance criteria are met, CI and security scans pass, release and rollback plans are ready, and smoke tests can verify critical flows. Deployments favor automation and include staging, production verification, and stakeholder communication. Failures trigger incident response and rollback procedures when necessary.

Finally, each sprint, release, milestone, or incident is followed by a **retrospective** that captures what went well, what could improve, and prioritized action items with owners and due dates. These actions become tracked work and are reviewed regularly, creating a repeatable cycle of delivery and institutional learning.
